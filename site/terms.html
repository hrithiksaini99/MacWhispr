Terms of use.

MacWhispr is an independently developed local dictation app by Hrithik Saini. These terms describe the current preview and its practical limits.

Last updated October 4, 2026

Using the preview

The developer preview is intended for personal evaluation on a compatible Mac. It is provided as available, without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation, a particular transcription time, or a specific level of accuracy.

Public distribution requires Apple Developer ID signing and notarization, which are pending for the current candidate. Check the setup guide for the actual availability and signing status of a download.

Review your words

Speech recognition can omit, repeat, or change words. Review the transcript before sending, publishing, or relying on it. Automatic paste may fail in secure fields or apps that do not accept synthesized keyboard input.

You are responsible for the recordings you make and how you use the resulting text. Obtain any permissions required to record other people.

Local requirements

MacWhispr needs a compatible operating system, sufficient model storage, and the permissions described in the setup guide. Transcription performance depends on your Mac, model, and recording length. Model downloads need internet access.
