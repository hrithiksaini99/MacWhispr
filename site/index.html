A little setup.
A simple daily ritual.

MacWhispr lives in your menu bar.
Here is how to get it ready.

  1. 1

    Get MacWhispr.

    Move MacWhispr into Applications. The packaged preview includes the whisper.cpp engine for Apple Silicon.

    Read the setup guide ↗
  2. 2

    Set up your voice.

    Choose a model in the menu bar and let it download. Allow Microphone access to record and Accessibility access to paste automatically.

  3. 3

    Speak where you write.

    Focus a text field, press your shortcut, and speak. Press again to finish. MacWhispr transcribes and pastes your words into that app.

    Control Space is the default, and it is yours to change.

A few useful details

Before you
find your flow.

Which Macs does MacWhispr support?+

MacWhispr targets macOS 13 or later. It has been built and exercised on Apple Silicon. Intel is supported by the package configuration and engine lookup paths, but has not yet been verified on an Intel Mac.

Does my audio leave my Mac?+

MacWhispr transcribes your recording locally through whisper.cpp. Audio is not uploaded for transcription. Temporary recordings are removed after processing, although an abrupt exit can leave temporary files behind. Your latest transcript stays on the clipboard.

Model downloads contact Hugging Face. See the privacy details for storage and logging information.

Do I need an internet connection?+

You need internet access to download MacWhispr and a model. The Apple Silicon preview includes the transcription engine. Once a model is installed, recording and transcription run locally without a network connection.

How does it put text into another app?+

MacWhispr remembers the app that was active when recording started, puts the transcript on the clipboard, and sends Command V with Accessibility permission. You can paste manually if that permission is unavailable. Some secure fields and apps may reject automated paste.

Can I personalize the shortcut?+

Yes. Open Shortcut in the menu and press a combination using Command, Option, or Control. MacWhispr checks that the shortcut is available before saving it. The menu also includes microphone, model, Toggle, and Push to Talk choices.

Is there an account, API key, or subscription?+

No. MacWhispr uses a local engine and model files. The developer preview does not require an account or paid API service. You will need space for a model, a compatible Mac, and the system permissions described in the setup guide.
