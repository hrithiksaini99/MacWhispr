Inside MacWhispr

A voice interface
with almost no interface.

MacWhispr was designed around a simple constraint: acknowledge every action without pulling attention away from the words. Explore how the capsule communicates state, speed, and trust.

The interaction loop

one human question.

Choose a moment to see what the capsule shows and why it changes. The preview is illustrative and never accesses your microphone.

MACWHISPRReady when you are

Immediate acknowledgement

Ready before the thought moves on.

The capsule appears close to the bottom edge, above the active app, without taking keyboard focus. The response is immediate so the shortcut feels connected to the result.

The visual language

Quiet enough for work.
Distinct enough to trust.

Every design choice supports focus, clarity, or perceived responsiveness.

Graphite surface

Present, without becoming a window.

The dark capsule separates itself from any document while its compact footprint keeps the work underneath visible.

Mint signal

One color carries the action.

Mint marks live audio, processing traces, and completion. The repeated cue makes each state readable at a glance.

Truthful motion

Activity without a false promise.

Directional traces show that transcription is active. They never pretend to know a completion percentage the engine cannot provide.

Built for the platform

A short path from
voice to words.

The app keeps the recording and transcription path on the Mac. AppKit owns system behavior, SwiftUI draws the capsule, and a pinned whisper.cpp engine turns temporary audio into text.
